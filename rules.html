House Rules

Welcome to Corridor Hill. Please read and follow these rules.

Keep the fridge clean

Clean and defrost your fridge regularly. Never leave food to spoil.

Respect quiet hours

Keep noise to a minimum between 22:00 and 06:00 for your neighbours.

Dispose of waste properly

Use the designated bins and separate recyclables from general waste.

Secure your room

Always lock your door and keep your key card safe. Report lost keys immediately.

Energy saving

Switch off lights, heaters and appliances when not in use.

Be a good neighbour

Keep shared spaces tidy and treat everyone with respect.
